About OG Cafe & Cream

Our Story & Mission

Family-Owned & Operated

Welcome to OG Cafe & Cream, where family tradition meets delicious treats. We are a family-operated ice cream and fresh hot donut shop dedicated to bringing joy and flavor to our community in Ocean Gate, New Jersey.

What started as a passion for making quality treats has grown into a beloved local destination. We've expanded our offerings to include breakfast sandwiches, hot beverages, and much more – all crafted with care and quality ingredients.

Every morning, we bake fresh donuts, prepare breakfast sandwiches, and serve expertly crafted beverages. Our ice cream is premium and creamy, made to delight every palate. Whether you're starting your day with us or treating yourself to something sweet, we're here to make your experience memorable.
